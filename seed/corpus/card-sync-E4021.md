# Card sync error E4021

## What it means

E4021 means Harbour could not sync a corporate card with the card issuer. Transactions may be delayed, but card authorisations usually still work.

## Fix

1. Open Settings, then Cards, and find the card marked Sync issue.
2. Choose Reconnect and complete the issuer login.
3. Wait five minutes and refresh the card activity.
4. If the error remains, remove and re-add the card.

## When to escalate

Escalate to the Cards team if the issuer reports the card as active but Harbour still shows E4021 after two reconnect attempts. Include the card's last four digits and the time of the last successful sync.
