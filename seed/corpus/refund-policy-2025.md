# Refund policy
Last updated: 4 March 2025. Owner: Billing team.

## Annual plans

Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of the initial purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

After the 14-day window, annual plans are non-refundable. Unused time is not prorated.

## Monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing period has started. You can cancel at any time to stop the next renewal.

## How to request a refund

Contact support from the workspace owner account and include the invoice number. We process approved refunds within five business days.

## Exceptions

Duplicate charges and billing errors are refunded in full, regardless of the refund window. Enterprise contracts follow the terms in the signed order form.
