# Plans and pricing
Prices are per user, per month, billed monthly unless stated.

## Team

Team costs $12 per user per month. It includes card issuing for up to 25 people and standard support.

## Business

Business costs $24 per user per month. It adds approvals, custom spend policies and priority support.

## Enterprise

Enterprise pricing is custom. It includes SSO, audit exports, a dedicated success manager and a 99.9% uptime agreement.
